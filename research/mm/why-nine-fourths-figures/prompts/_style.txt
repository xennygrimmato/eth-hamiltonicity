Draw one page of a hand-drawn programming/maths zine in the style of "drawings jvns" (drawings.jvns.ca, Julia Evans' wizard zines comics).

Style rules:
- Looks drawn by hand with a black felt-tip pen on a plain white page. Slightly wobbly lines, nothing computer-perfect.
- All text is neat, friendly, hand-lettered handwriting (mostly lowercase), large and easy to read.
- A big hand-lettered title at the top inside a hand-drawn rounded box.
- The page is split into a few hand-drawn panels/boxes with thick wobbly black borders.
- Simple stick-figure people with round heads, dot eyes and a small smile, with speech bubbles and thought bubbles.
- Little hand-drawn arrows pointing from notes to the things they explain.
- Limited flat colour: black ink plus a few highlights in warm pink, orange and light purple (highlighter-style fills behind key words, coloured lines in diagrams). No gradients, no shading, no photorealism, no 3D.
- Curious, cheerful, plain-spoken tone.
- Spell every word exactly as given. Write every formula and number exactly as given, with nothing added. Do not add extra text, logos, signatures, URLs or page numbers.
- Portrait page.

This work was produced by Vaibhav Tulsyan in sessions with Claude Code (Anthropic). The mathematical
arguments, the Lean 4 formalisation and the text were drafted by Claude under my direction. I chose the
problems, directed the research, and reviewed the statements of the main theorems together with the
paper's own account of what is machine-checked, what is proved on paper, what is assumed and what is
checked numerically. Every result the paper labels as machine-checked is checked in Lean 4, on top of the
Anthropic manuscript's own Lean development: the three theorems EthFollowup.ModP.repNum_TM_rate,
EthFollowup.ModP.diagRep_TM_rate and EthFollowup.ModP.tunnell_TM_rate in the module
EthFollowup.ModP.FinalRep (a finite-alphabet Mathlib Turing machine outputs the representation number of a
fixed positive-definite form, of a diagonal form, and Tunnell's test bit, within the stated step bound).
#print axioms shows only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, no new module contains sorry or
native_decide, and a fresh leanchecker replay of the dependency closure of EthFollowup.ModP.FinalRep passed
on 5 November 2026. The Lean sources are public at https://github.com/xennygrimmato/eth-hamiltonicity
(directory lean-bridge-anthropic/bridge/EthFollowup/ModP: RepQF, SlpDesc, RepDesc, CntPad, RepPoly, RepPkg,
FinalRep), committed at 3e181e776a38ada042c0c3318c0d66cc2ccf498b, the commit the paper cites, and unchanged
at b7b73525c1be026efdcf92c95a97c26e37cbeaf1. The consequences for L-values (Tunnell's Theorem 3), for central
values of imaginary quadratic twists (Bocherer-Schulze-Pillot) and, conditionally on OpenAI's unrefereed
full-BSD result, for Tate-Shafarevich groups are proved on paper only and are labelled as such; they were
checked numerically with PARI/GP and Python on small ranges, and these checks are not part of any proof.
One independent AI referee pass was carried out and its corrections were made. No human expert has yet
reviewed the work. All results are conditional on Anthropic's claimed refutation of the Exponential Time
Hypothesis, which is under review.

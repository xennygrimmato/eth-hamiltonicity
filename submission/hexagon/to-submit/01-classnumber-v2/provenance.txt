This work was produced by Vaibhav Tulsyan in sessions with Claude Code (Anthropic). The mathematical
arguments, the Lean 4 formalisation and the text were drafted by Claude under my direction. I chose the
problems, directed the research, and reviewed the statements of the main theorems together with the
paper's own account of what is proved, what is assumed, what is checked numerically and what is not
formalised. Every theorem the paper claims is machine-checked in Lean 4 on top of the Anthropic
manuscript's own Lean development: the axiom audit shows only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound,
and fresh leanchecker replays of the full dependency closure passed. Computations, written proofs and
informal remarks are labelled as such in the paper and are not claimed as machine-checked. The Lean
sources are public at https://github.com/xennygrimmato/eth-hamiltonicity. Independent AI referee passes,
exact numerical checks and a citation audit were carried out; the definitions a reader must check to
trust the formal statements are listed in the paper. Version 2 was prepared after an AI-run novelty
audit (5 November 2026) found that two results in OpenAI's catalogue (github.com/openai/math, entries 003
and 142) already give polynomial time for the least quadratic non-residue and for square roots modulo a
prime. Claude drafted the corrections, which change only the text: the title, the abstract, the
comparisons with earlier work, the related work and the limitations, plus a derivation on paper of the
least square root modulo a composite number from deterministic factoring, which is labelled as not
formalised. The Lean development and its theorems are unchanged from version 1. No human expert has yet
reviewed the work. All results are conditional on the claimed refutations of the Exponential Time
Hypothesis, which are under review.

This work was produced by Vaibhav Tulsyan in sessions with Claude Code (Anthropic). The mathematical
arguments, the Lean 4 formalisation and the text were drafted by Claude under my direction. I chose the
problems, directed the research, and reviewed the statements of the main theorems together with the
paper's own account of what is machine-checked, what is proved on paper, what is assumed and what is
checked numerically. The paper cites 45 Lean 4 theorems in 16 modules of the directory
lean/EthFollowup/Barriers of the public repository https://github.com/xennygrimmato/eth-hamiltonicity
(commit 946c49993b78aa163fcac3f8cb78ce351dd5dcc6; the Lean files are committed and unchanged there): RankBounds.lean, RowCut.lean,

They are written on Mathlib only and do not use the Anthropic or OpenAI developments; the
axiom audit of 5 November 2026 (#print axioms for all 251 theorem declarations of the directory, all 16
modules imported in one file) shows only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, and no module
contains sorry or native_decide. The step from a network to a factorised matrix, the cost-to-rank step,
the Tutte barrier, the full-level bound 3/8, the grouping and covering caps, the template corollaries
and the meta-barrier are proved on paper only and are labelled as such. The research notes at that commit
include the corrections made for this version: the repaired covering proof in
research/engine2-probabilistic.md section 7, the corrected conditional cap in research/engine2-algebraic.md M6.2,
and the re-run check research/scripts/engine2p_covering.py. Independent AI referee passes, an overlap comparison with Majdoddin
(arXiv:2610.41622) and Dias-Lima (arXiv:2611.00517), and a citation audit were carried out. No human
expert has yet reviewed the work. The barriers are statements about named models of computation; they
do not depend on the claimed refutations of the Exponential Time Hypothesis being correct.

Title (in the rounded box at the top): "equal arms are the best case"

Panel 1 (top left): a lopsided Y-shaped tripod with one very long arm and two short arms. A stick figure next to it looks unimpressed. Caption: "lopsided arms: smaller ratio"

Panel 2 (top right): a balanced Y-shaped tripod with three equal arms. A stick figure next to it looks happy. Caption: "equal arms: the biggest ratio"

Panel 4 (bottom, wide, highlighted): text: "so the ratio can never exceed". Below it, written large:

    2 ÷ (1 − 1/9) = 9/4
